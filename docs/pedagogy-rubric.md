# LearnHives Pedagogy Rubric

Status key: **ACCEPTED** = agreed rule. All rules below are ACCEPTED.

## Stages and limits

| Stage | Age | Max words per card | Quiz choices | Cards per lesson | Max minutes per session |
|-------|-----|--------------------|--------------|------------------|-------------------------|
| Seedling | 2–3 | 1 | 2 | 5 | 5 |
| Sprout | 3–4 | 1–2 | 3 | 8 | 7 |
| Blossom | 4–5 | phrase ≤5 | 3 | 10–12 | 10 |
| Bloom | 5–6 | sentence ≤8 | 4 | 12–16 | 12 |

- Stages (ACCEPTED): Seedling 2–3, Sprout 3–4, Blossom 4–5, Bloom 5–6.
- Max words per card (ACCEPTED): 1 / 1–2 / phrase ≤5 / sentence ≤8.
- Quiz choices (ACCEPTED): 2 / 3 / 3 / 4.
- Cards per lesson (ACCEPTED): 5 / 8 / 10–12 / 12–16.
- Max minutes per session (ACCEPTED): 5 / 7 / 10 / 12.

## Cultural

- (ACCEPTED) No pig as animal or food.
- (ACCEPTED) No pork or beef as food.
- (ACCEPTED) British English.
- (ACCEPTED) No alcohol.
- (ACCEPTED) Modest clothing.
- (ACCEPTED) Mix of Arabic, Indian and international names.
- (ACCEPTED) Festivals neutral, or Eid and Diwali together.

## Never appear (ACCEPTED)

- Scary content
- Weapons or violence
- Brands or junk food
- Gender stereotypes
- Shaming wrong answers
- Ranking children
- Buzz asking personal details
- External links or ads

## Buzz tone (ACCEPTED)

- Warm, playful, brief.
- Maximum 2 short sentences.
- Use words the stage knows.
- End on encouragement.
- Never harsh correction.

## Card backs (ACCEPTED)

- Seedling and Sprout: name and sound only. Facts go to Buzz and the story.

## Story limits (ACCEPTED)

| Stage | Max sentences |
|-------|---------------|
| Seedling | 3 short |
| Sprout | 4 |
| Blossom | 6 |
| Bloom | 8 |

## Animals (ACCEPTED)

- Baby animal names: just "baby" below Blossom.
- Dog is allowed at all stages.
- Predators that look fierce (crocodile, wolf): Blossom and Bloom only.
- No hunting or eating-prey wording for any animal.
